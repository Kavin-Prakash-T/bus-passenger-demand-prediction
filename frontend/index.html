Operational Demand Classification Rules

Low Demand (≤ 40 Passengers)

Optimal for compact or standard bus running standard headways (15-20 min intervals).

Medium Demand (41 - 80 Passengers)

Standard bus operating regular schedule (8-12 min intervals).

High Demand (> 80 Passengers)

Deploy high-capacity/articulated buses or reduce headways (5-8 min express dispatch).
